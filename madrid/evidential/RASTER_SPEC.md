RASTER EXPERIMENT SPEC v1 (draft) - three-way relaxation comparison (probabilistic / possibilistic / evidential)
1 Cases: the 236 panel dates of the Madrid LOSO study; split 70 / 70 / 96 days (train / validation / final); same dates, stations and truth for all methods.
2 Satellite evidence: COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_NO2, tropospheric column (mol/m2). Same-day images combined by pixel-wise mean of valid observations (count kept). Native product grid (fixed; no per-date grid). Missing stays missing; no interpolation across dates.
3 Evidence units: for each station-date the centre cell and its 8 Moore neighbours (3x3 window), taken on the native grid; need >=4 valid neighbours (sensitivity: >=6). Cells are an oversampled gridded neighbourhood, NOT 8 independent measurements (TROPOMI nadir footprint ~3.5x5.5 km): hence cautious pooling.
4 Satellite-to-surface calibration (frozen from days 1-70 only, satellite only, no same-day ground at test time): ground = a + b*column (centre cell), residual RMSE s_cal (training). Four classes <15,15-30,30-45,>=45 ug/m3; centroids from training. Initial pixel evidence: same possibility contour (eta fixed) -> prob / poss / consonant BBA for all methods.
5 Compatibility R(k,l)=exp(-(c_k-c_l)^2/(2 s^2)), s = s_cal (NOT the 6.95 of the fusion study). rho from {0.5,0.8,0.95}, common to evidential, chosen on validation Brier of BetP only.
6 Methods compared: no relaxation; Rosenfeld probabilistic; possibilistic (frozen v1 spec); evidential primary (cautious pool + cautious centre, K=4 implementation evid_k.py), comparators Dempster / cautious pool + Dempster centre.
7 Scoring: station-date pairs, centre-cell value only (no interpolation). Accuracy, multiclass Brier and ECE of (normalised) BetP always with m(empty); coverage vs set size; risk-coverage; conflict frequency; paired 7-day block bootstrap keeping all stations of a date together; report n pairs, unique dates, stations, blocks. 24 stations are not 24 independent replications.
8 Limits to state: column vs surface mismatch; daytime overpass vs daily mean; 1.1 km grid vs 3.5x5.5 km footprint; ground-calibration uses 24 stations.
9 First run: TEST export (5 dates) to verify grid alignment, valid-neighbour counts, station matching; then the full export.
10 CDD-evidential (second evidential method, data-driven dependence): canonical weights of every BBA (centre and 8 neighbours, k=9 sources) raised to lambda=1/(1+(k-1)*rho_bar), then multiplied (conjunctive rule on weights), m(empty) retained. rho_bar = mean pairwise correlation of calibration errors (ground - calibrated satellite) among the 9 cells of a window, estimated on days 1-70 only, clipped to [0,1]; frozen before validation. Heuristic (exact only for equicorrelated Gaussian errors); not duplicate-invariant, unlike the cautious primary; report both. For simple supports this is the CDD rule of Paper T (Prop. cdd).
11 Optional sensitivity: Frank t-norm family on canonical weights (Denoeux 2008, Sec. 5; s=0 cautious, s=1 TBM conjunctive); only values of s that keep a valid belief function (between product and minimum); s grid {0, 0.1, 0.5, 1} on validation; Kallel & Le Hegarat-Mascle and Chebbah et al. rules only if time allows.
12 Report the measured rho_bar and the resulting lambda in the paper, and the fraction of windows with m(empty) above the validation threshold.
